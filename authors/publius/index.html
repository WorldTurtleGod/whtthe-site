Scientists Built a Replacement Glue for Aging Eggs. It Works in Mice. Humans Are Years Away.

In 17-month-old mice it lifted the share of eggs with no premature splitting from 46.7% to 74.1%, about what young mice show. Outside experts call it clever and warn nobody has fertilized a treated egg yet.
