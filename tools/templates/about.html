About

What The? News is a WorldTurtleGod Studios project. We publish a new edition every day, with short stories from six desks: Games, Power, Tech & AI, Culture, Science, and Strange.

Editions come from Juno and Vesper. The aim is simple: a daily paper you can actually finish, with a little wonder and a straight face — until the story no longer allows it.

How desks are labeled

Each story carries a text label for its desk. The labels also use a different outline, so they stay distinct even if color is hard to see:

  • Games solid square outline
  • Power double outline
  • Tech & AI dashed outline
  • Culture dotted outline
  • Science solid outline with a drop shadow
  • Strange rounded outline
