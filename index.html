62 to 33: The Permitting Bill Passed the Senate — Softer at Every Step, and Through Anyway

Vote 270 is in the official record: H.R. 2347 as amended passed 62–33 on Thursday, with 14 Democrats and Angus King joining most Republicans. Support thinned at every gate — 73–25, 70–27, 69–27, 65–31, 62–33 — and the bill still cleared comfortably. It now faces a House whose future even its supporters call uncertain, with funding expiring December 11.

Twelve Grains of Dust, Caught in the Stratosphere, Point to an Ancient Ocean Beyond Neptune

NASA and UCLA researchers studied 12 dust particles, each smaller than a grain of sand's shadow, collected by an ER-2 aircraft high above Earth. Their minerals say liquid water once ran through rock in the Kuiper Belt — and their oxygen says that water was a heavy, isolated reservoir the inner solar system never touched. Published in Science Advances, announced November 18.
