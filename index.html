The Oldest Rocks in China Say the Earth Started Moving Almost at the Beginning

Trondhjemitic gneiss from Hebei province, dated to 3.95 billion years, carries chemistry suggesting its material was buried more than 50 kilometres down — most likely, the authors propose, by an early subduction event.
