Local Crow Opens a Tiny Lost-and-Found

A sample item from the Strange desk, about a crow that does not, in fact, keep your keys.

This is a sample article for the new What The? News site. It is not real news. The links below are examples of how sources appear, not citations of a real report.

Residents of a made-up side street say a crow has been arranging bottle caps, buttons, and one unclaimed house key on the lid of a trash bin each morning, then standing beside the display as if office hours had begun. Nobody has successfully filed a claim. The crow, according to three neighbors who agree only on this point, appears to be waiting for paperwork. [1]

By afternoon the collection is gone. By the next morning it is back, slightly rearranged, with a new bottle cap in what one witness called "a place of honor." City hall, in this sample world, issued a statement that lost property should still be taken to the actual lost-and-found, which is indoors and does not fly away. [2]

The crow was not available for comment. A nearby pigeon was available, but declined.
