Ella Langley Went Six for Six at the CMAs. Taylor Swift Went Home Empty-Handed.

The 60th CMA Awards in Nashville belonged to "Choosin' Texas." Langley won entertainer of the year on her first try, and her song beat Swift's return to the country field.

By Publius·Thursday, November 19, 2026·Edition 65

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

Ella Langley walked into Wednesday night's CMA Awards with nine nominations and walked out with six trophies, every category in which she was the named nominee. [1]

The 60th CMA Awards were held Nov. 18 at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, hosted by Lainey Wilson. [1][2] The Country Music Association says Langley set a record for the most CMA wins by anyone in a single year. [2] Langley won entertainer of the year, the show's top prize, the first year she was ever nominated for it. She beat Luke Combs, Cody Johnson, Chris Stapleton and Lainey Wilson. [1] She also took female vocalist of the year, album of the year for "Dandelion," and single, song and music video of the year for "Choosin' Texas." [1][2]

Miranda Lambert rode along. She co-produced the album and the single and co-wrote "Choosin' Texas," which earned her three trophies on the night, according to Billboard. [1] Billboard reported the only other multiple winners were also Langley's collaborators. [1]

The night's other headline was a loss. Taylor Swift was nominated for single of the year for "I Knew It, I Knew You," a song from Disney and Pixar's "Toy Story 5." It was her first CMA nomination since 2022, and "Choosin' Texas" beat it. [3] USA Today reported that the Swift song topped country radio in September, nearly 12 years after she moved from country to pop with "1989." [3] Her career total stays at 12 CMA Awards, including entertainer of the year in 2009 and 2011 and the Pinnacle Award. [3] She still showed up to sing, performing a mashup of "I Knew It, I Knew You" and her early hit "Our Song," with Keith Urban. [3]

The rest of the winners, per the CMA and Billboard: [1][2]

  • Male vocalist: Chris Stapleton, his ninth win in the category, extending his record
  • New artist: Stephen Wilson Jr., his first CMA Award
  • Vocal group: The Red Clay Strays, for the second year running
  • Vocal duo: Brooks & Dunn, this year's Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement honorees, who remain the most-awarded act in CMA history with 21 wins
  • Musical event: "McArthur," by HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw, giving McGraw a category-record fifth win
  • Musician: Charlie Worsham, guitar, his second win

Swift's own 2009 night is the obvious comparison: that year she won all four categories she was nominated in and, at 19, became the youngest entertainer of the year. [3] Langley topped it by two.

The winners are picked by the CMA's 7,000-plus voting members, a mix of artists, songwriters, producers, touring workers and industry executives, and the ballots are tabulated by Deloitte, according to the association. [2] The show aired live on ABC and streams on Hulu and Disney+. [2]
