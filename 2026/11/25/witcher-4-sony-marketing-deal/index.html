The Witcher 4 Just Signed With PlayStation. CD Projekt Says Expect a GTA VI-Sized Leap.

On its third-quarter earnings call, CD Projekt Red said it signed new agreements with Sony covering PlayStation, PlayStation Plus and the coming Witcher 4 marketing campaign. The game is still due in 2028.

By Publius·Wednesday, November 25, 2026·Edition 71

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

The Witcher 4 has a marketing partner, and it is Sony.

On CD Projekt Red's third-quarter earnings call Tuesday, chief financial officer Piotr Nielubowicz said the Polish studio had, "just today," signed "new partnership agreements with Sony Interactive Entertainment, covering the presence of our games on PlayStation and in PlayStation Plus subscriptions as well as our collaboration during the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign." He said the deal "will support our business and financial outlook in this and future years." [1]

That is all the company put on the record. The transcript gives no price, no length for the deal and no word on exclusive content. [1] VGTimes reported that the studio stressed the marketing deal does not include timed console exclusivity, and that financial terms and duration were not disclosed. [2] The published transcript itself does not address exclusivity either way, so treat that point as VGTimes's account until CD Projekt or Sony says more. [1][2] Game Unc noted it is the same kind of arrangement Sony struck for Grand Theft Auto VI. [3]

The date did not move. Asked about The Witcher 4 "still" being set for 2028, joint CEO Michał Nowakowski answered: "there's no 'still'; we just keep The Witcher 4 premiere for 2028." [1] A third expansion for The Witcher 3, Songs of the Past, is scheduled for 2027. [1]

GTA VI hung over the whole call. Asked whether The Witcher 4 could make the same jump over The Witcher 3 that GTA VI made over GTA V, Nowakowski said "this is a call about us, not about Rockstar," then answered anyway: "you should expect us to make a much bigger jump between TW3 and TW4." [1] He said GTA VI had not changed the studio's ambitions for The Witcher 4 or Cyberpunk 2, calling them "ambition-driven developments" rather than budget-driven ones, while adding that "the budgets are not indefinite." [1]

He also admitted GTA VI took a bite. On PlayStation and Xbox, he said, "around 25% of the players engaged themselves with GTA VI – initially," but CD Projekt saw no impact on PC, where GTA VI has not launched, and expects no material effect on fourth-quarter sales. [1]

The numbers behind the talk: third-quarter revenue topped 208 million zloty and net profit reached 109 million zloty, according to the transcript. Revenue from The Witcher games rose 44% year over year, helped by The Witcher 3 Remastered, released September 29. [1] The company also announced an advance dividend of 1.20 zloty per share, nearly 120 million zloty in total, payable December 29. [1] On Cyberpunk 2, the studio said it plans about 200 developers by the end of this year and about 320 by the end of next. [1]

Commentary. "Marketing partnership" is a polite phrase. When GTA VI signed with Sony, PlayStation got to call itself the best place to play it. Players on Xbox and PC deserve a straight answer, before 2028, on whether any Witcher 4 content or timing will be held back from them. CD Projekt built its name on generous free updates and no-nonsense DRM-free sales on GOG. A deal this big should come with the same plain dealing.
