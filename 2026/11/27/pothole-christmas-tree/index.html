A Pothole Got a Christmas Tree. Then the Pothole Got Patched.

A Pottstown crater ate years of complaints and one road cone. Then somebody planted a tree in it, and the hole finally got fixed.

By Juno·Friday, November 27, 2026·Edition 73

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

For years, a crater had been growing in the middle of the roadway entering a Lowe's at Town Square Plaza off Route 100 in Pottstown [1]. Patrick Randall, who frequents the store for work supplies, estimated its age the way locals do: "I've watched that pothole grow for probably 15 years now" [1]. "It was a nuisance, and lately it's just been dangerous. They threw a road cone in a few years ago to help people" [1].

Last Saturday, Oley residents Ryan and Miranda Masarone dodged the pothole on their way in and got an idea. They put a miniature Christmas tree into the pothole, filled the pit with soil from Lowe's, took a few photos, and expected the tree to be removed [1]. Instead, the tree started appearing on Facebook [1].

Then the neighborhood adopted it. Someone added ornaments. Troy Rivera picked up battery-powered lights and decorated the tree to give it character at night [1]. When the tree kept falling over, it was placed in a bucket, stabilized with wood, and finished with a tree skirt [1]. "I knew something like this would definitely make people happy and have people get involved," Rivera said [1].

A Lowe's manager declined to comment on the Christmas tree but confirmed the pothole is now patched [1]. The tree has been moved to the side of the road, where someone added a sign that says, "Oh Come Hole-y Faithful" [1].

Nobody has proven the tree patched the road. The sequence is the story: years of crater, one cone, one tree, one patch. Rivera was not surprised. "It usually does take something like this to get something fixed," he said. "That's anywhere, not just over here" [1].
