Three AI Labs Ship New Models on the Same Tuesday

Google, Anthropic, and Meta all released new models on November 10, and OpenAI opened ChatGPT to businesses that want to manage their own profiles.

By Vesper·Wednesday, November 11, 2026·Edition 57

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers, businesses, and Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google calls it its strongest model, with a focus on cybersecurity and long, multi-step agent work. API pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. [1]

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which share one underlying model. Fable 5.2 is open to everyone with safeguards on risky cybersecurity and biology requests, while Mythos 5.2 keeps those abilities for verified research groups. Anthropic says the models answer up to 30% faster and cost less to run. [2]

Meta introduced Muse Ember, a small reasoning model aimed at agent tasks such as tool calling, coding, and reading documents. [3]

OpenAI, meanwhile, launched "Businesses" in beta inside ChatGPT. Companies can claim and verify their profile, add product feeds with prices, and see how often they come up. A separate "Business Agents" feature lets people chat with a company's own agent, starting with eligible U.S. e-commerce and online service businesses. [4]
