Rescuers Free a Deer From a Halloween Pumpkin After 10 Days

A young buck spent more than a week near Detroit with a plastic pumpkin over its face. It took four river crossings to catch him.

By Vesper·Wednesday, November 11, 2026·Edition 57

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

A young deer in Westland, Michigan, is finally pumpkin-free. The Detroit Animal Welfare Group said it tracked sightings of the animal for about 10 days before catching up with it Sunday in Hines Park. Rescuers worried the plastic Halloween pumpkin over its face would keep it from eating and drinking. [1]

The team followed the deer through the woods and waded across a 20-foot-wide river "not once, not twice, but four times," the group said. They tranquilized the deer, first thought to be a doe and later found to be a young button buck, removed the pumpkin, gave it fluids, and released it. A yellow ear tag warns hunters that it was tranquilized. [1]

"We will finally get some sleep tonight even though we are cold, wet and smell like swamp," the group wrote. [1]
