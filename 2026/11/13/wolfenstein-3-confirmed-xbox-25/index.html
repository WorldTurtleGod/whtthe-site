Wolfenstein 3 Is Real — Xbox's 25th Birthday Brought the News Fans Waited Nine Years For

MachineGames is officially making the next Wolfenstein, with BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker back. No trailer, no date — just the confirmation.

By Juno·Friday, November 13, 2026·Edition 59

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

Nine years after Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus, the next one is finally, officially happening.

Xbox CEO Asha Sharma announced on X on Nov. 12 that Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames, as Xbox closed out its eight-city FanFest tour in Seattle for its 25th anniversary. MachineGames confirmed it separately, posting a script page and a motion-capture photo: actors Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson are reprising their roles as BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker. [1][2]

That is the whole announcement. No release date, no platforms, no trailer, no subtitle. Just the confirmation, and the cast. [1]

For a series that has been in limbo since 2017, the confirmation is the news. Wolfenstein is one of gaming's most important franchises — the 2014 reboot made first-person shooters about something again, and The New Colossus swung for the fences with a story about America under occupation that most big-budget games would not touch. MachineGames has spent the years since on Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, which was very good and very much not Wolfenstein. Now the team is back on the game that made its name, with the two leads intact. [1]

The announcement came at the end of a packed FanFest night. Halo: Campaign Evolved gets Firefight, the co-op survival mode, in a free Winter Update. DOOM: The Dark Ages gets Slaughter Mode on Nov. 16, free for Revelations DLC and Premium owners. Fable showed new quest gameplay. Xbox Backward Compatibility on PC now includes 25 original Xbox games, including Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic and The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind. And id Software confirmed its next project is in development, directed again by Hugo Martin. [1]

Xbox turns 25 officially on Sunday, Nov. 15. The birthday present for its players was a list of things coming and one big thing confirmed: the Blazkowicz story is not finished. After nine years of silence, that is enough — for now.
