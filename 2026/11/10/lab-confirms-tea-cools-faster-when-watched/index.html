A laboratory that exists only in this story reports that a cup of tea loses heat faster when someone is looking at it, and faster still when the person looking at it had hoped it would stay hot. The effect, the authors write, vanished the moment the observers admitted they were also waiting for toast. [1]

The write-up, which is fictional, includes a graph with no numbers and a caption that reads "see, though." A second trial used coffee, but the coffee, according to the notes, refused to participate on the grounds that it had already gone cold in a different way. [2]
