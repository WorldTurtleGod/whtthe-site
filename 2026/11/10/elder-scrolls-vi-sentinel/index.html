After Eight Years, Elder Scrolls VI Finally Has a Name

Bethesda ended the longest silence in modern gaming with a single word: Sentinel. No gameplay, no date — just a name, a place, and a promise the game still exists.

By Juno·Tuesday, November 10, 2026·Edition 55

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

On Tuesday, November 3, Bethesda Game Studios ended the longest silence in modern gaming with a single word: Sentinel. The full title, revealed at the end of a video marking the studio's 40th anniversary, is The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel. [1]

The game was first teased at E3 in June 2018 — a landscape, a logo, nothing else. Eight years later, players finally have a name and a tease of a place. The roughly one-minute teaser ends with a quote from Yazar, Moth Priest of High Queen Iradne of Hammerfell, dated 4E 206, five years after Skyrim's setting in 4E 201. Bethesda's own post said the reveal covered "the name and location," which most outlets read as confirming Hammerfell — though at least one outlet cautioned that Bethesda has not flatly announced the setting beyond the teaser itself, so read the location as pointed to, not sworn to in a press release. [2]

Bethesda's statement, in full, is worth quoting because it is the whole announcement: "Thank you for 40 years of shared memories. We can't wait to make even more together with The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel. Development continues, and while it'll be some time before we're ready to share more, it feels great to finally reveal the name and location with you."

What was not announced matters as much as what was. No gameplay. No release date or window. No platform list. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reports the game is still more than two years away — that is his reporting, not a Bethesda date, and it points to 2028 at the earliest. [3]

That is the honest shape of this news: after eight years of nothing, Bethesda gave fans a name to argue about and a date on a fictional calendar to decode. It is not a game yet. It is a promise that the game still exists — and for a series whose last main entry turns fifteen on November 11, that promise was overdue.
