Super Meat Boy Is Coming Back "Again+." One of the Two Men Who Made It Says It's All News to Him.

Team Meat announced a top-to-bottom remake of its 2010 platformer, with new art, music and a secret "+" it won't explain. Co-creator Edmund McMillen, who left the studio years ago, isn't involved.

By Herodotus·Monday, November 16, 2026·Edition 62

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

Every old song gets a re-recording sooner or later. Super Meat Boy, the 2010 platformer about a skinless boy who leaves a smear of himself on every wall, is getting its own.

Team Meat announced Super Meat Boy Again+ for PC on Steam. The studio describes it as "everything you remember from Super Meat Boy presented through the wonderful lens of nostalgia with stuff added to it," with new art, music, cutscenes, levels, secrets and unlockables. [1][2] The Steam page promises more than 300 levels, a soundtrack by the original composer, Danny B, and, in its own words, "Saws." [1] There is no release date and no price yet. [1][2]

The "+" is a deliberate mystery. Team Meat says that since 2010 "all the secrets are known, all the levels are mastered," so it has to give veterans "something to pull their hair out over," and it won't spoil what that is. [1]

The reveal came at a Sweet 16 Birthday VIP event on November 14, Polygon reported. [3] It also reopened an old seam. Edmund McMillen co-founded Team Meat and designed the original game, but he had left the studio by 2017, and he is not part of the remake. [3] Asked on X whether he had anything to do with it, McMillen replied, "It's all news to me man." [3] Co-founder and programmer Tommy Refenes has run Team Meat as CEO since, releasing Super Meat Boy Forever, Dr. Fetus' Mean Meat Machine and this year's Super Meat Boy 3D. [3] McMillen, for his part, had one of the year's biggest indie hits on PC with the cat-breeding tactics game Mewgenics. [3]

The Steam pitch to newcomers is blunt and a little self-aware: a hard but fair platformer "that you may have missed because it came out like four years after YouTube was invented." [1]
