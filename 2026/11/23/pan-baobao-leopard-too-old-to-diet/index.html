China's Famous Chubby Leopard Is 19. His Zoo Says He's Done Dieting.

Pan Baobao went viral for his belly, lost the weight, and now looks round again. Keepers at Panzhihua Zoo say at his age the goal is health, not a slimmer figure, and he gets more than a pound of beef a day.

By Publius·Monday, November 23, 2026·Edition 69

Share

Copied Share on X Facebook Reddit Email

Some celebrities make a comeback. Pan Baobao made a comeback in size.

The 19-year-old leopard at Panzhihua Zoo in Sichuan Province, southwestern China, became an internet star in March 2024 after visitors posted videos of his round belly and stretched-out spots. [1][2] The zoo put him on a weight-loss program, and it worked. [1][3] Now visitors say he looks round again, and the zoo says it isn't going to do anything about it.

What the zoo said. Chinese outlet Jiupai News reached the zoo on Nov. 17. A staff member said the leopard is old now and can't lose weight, and that the best thing is to keep him stable and healthy. "Just like people when they get old, you can see his skin is loose," the worker said, in our translation. [3] In the English version of the comments, reported by The Sun and repeated by WION, keeper Zhang Songwen said: "It's not that we're not controlling its diet, but it's getting old and shouldn't be losing weight. Animals are actually like people. As they get older, their skin becomes loose." [1][2]

The menu is not small. Pan Baobao eats more than a jin, about a pound, of beef a day, with chicken swapped in for balance, according to Jiupai News. [3] The Sun put it at more than 500 grams of beef daily. [1] A recent checkup found him healthy, and keepers say his weight has not changed much from before. [1][3] The zoo worker said they can't weigh him right now, so they judge his condition by whether he keeps a steady weight and stays in good spirits. [3]

He isn't just lying around. Keepers put a big ball in his enclosure to play with and push him to move more. [1][3]

From viral cat to tourism official. His fame drew visitors from across China, The Sun reported. [1] In April 2025, local tourism authorities named him an honorary tourism director. [1][2] Chinese fans call him "Officer Leopard," after his resemblance to the chubby cheetah police officer in the movie Zootopia, Jiupai News noted. [3] Zoo staff told Red Star News in February 2025 that the leopard was then 16 or 17, about 70 or 80 in human years, and that slimming down would help him live longer. [3]

Not everyone is charmed. After the viral attention, critics accused the zoo of fattening its animals on purpose to create a social media star like the baby hippo Moo Deng, The Sun reported. [1] South Korea's Yonhap News TV, citing The Sun, said the zoo is also known for an unusually plump bear and peacocks. [4] The zoo has said its veterinarians examine the animals regularly and that their diets include vegetables, fruit, grains and supplements, according to Jiupai News. [3]

The story first ran in Chinese media Nov. 17 and spread through English, Spanish and Korean outlets over the weekend. [1][3][4][5]

Commentary. A zoo deciding an old animal has earned his dinner isn't usually news. The internet made this cat famous for his gut, made the zoo put him on a diet, and is now arguing about his gut again. The keepers' answer is that a healthy old cat matters more than a slim one. They're the ones who see him every day, and they have the vet records. Online commenters who never visited Sichuan don't get a vote, and the keepers were right not to give them one.
