Goldenvoice revealed the Coachella 2027 lineup on Tuesday, Nov. 10. The headliners: Dua Lipa on Fridays, Tame Impala on Saturdays, SZA on Sundays. The festival runs two weekends, April 9–11 and April 16–18, 2027, at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California. [1][2]

It is a woman-centered top of the bill — two of the three headliners are women, and the Desert Sun's write-up put it plainly: "Coachella 2027 is for the girls." [1] Dua Lipa is on the Coachella lineup for the first time — she was a surprise guest in Martin Garrix's 2017 set. SZA first played her own set in the Gobi Tent in 2016 and made her main-stage debut in 2018. Tame Impala headlined in 2019. [1]

But the biggest name on the poster might be on the second line. Madonna — Madonna Louise Ciccone, the Queen of Pop herself — will perform "in the dance tent" on Fridays, according to the official lineup poster. [1] This is her first billed Coachella lineup slot since 2006, when she played a mid-day, 50-minute Sahara tent set, arrived about 25 minutes late, and played only six songs. She has appeared since as a surprise guest — in Drake's set in 2015 and with Sabrina Carpenter in 2026 — but never as a billed act. [1] The distinction matters: a surprise guest is a favor; a billed slot is a statement.

Also on the bill: Daddy Yankee ("in the desert"), Robyn, Zara Larsson, Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Don Toliver, Kehlani, Dom Dolla, Four Tet, NCT, Diljit Dosanjh, JISOO, and Justice, billed as "The Bunker Debut." [1][2] The headliner trio matched late-October rumors from TMZ and Complex; Goldenvoice had declined comment on Oct. 30. [1]

Tickets: the Amex presale opens at noon PT today, Thursday, Nov. 12; general passes go on sale at noon PT Friday, Nov. 13, at coachella.com. [1][2] The lineup drop drove Coachella to #9 on X Trending on Nov. 11. [4]

A lineup announcement is not a show, and no one should pretend otherwise — this is a poster, not a performance, and the 2006 Madonna set is a reminder that a big name in a tent can still be a short, late, six-song affair. But as posters go, this one is doing something Coachella has rarely done: it put women at the top and then put the most famous woman in pop history in the dance tent underneath them. April is a long way off. The presale starts today.
