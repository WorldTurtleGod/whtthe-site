Sony has stopped work on Fairgames, the online heist shooter Haven Studios announced in 2023. Bloomberg reported that an internal email from two PlayStation executives said a recent test drew "mixed" player feedback and retention that fell short of their goals, in what they called a tough market for player-versus-player shooters. PlayStation told Bloomberg there will be no layoffs, and Haven will move on to support other PlayStation Studios teams. [1]

On the same day, Sony's website began listing more than 100 million PS5 consoles shipped worldwide as of November 10. That count is consoles shipped to stores, not consoles bought by players. It stood at 97.9 million on September 30. [1]

Sony is also retiring the PlayStation Plus app for PC on Thursday, December 10. The company says fewer than 0.4% of eligible Premium members were using it. PS5 and PS4 memberships are not affected. [1]

And the elephant in every store: Grand Theft Auto VI is now the most pre-ordered game ever on both the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. Pre-loading is open on PS5, and the game unlocks at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19. [1]

In better news for patient players, FromSoftware turned the Dark Souls II and Dark Souls III servers back on today after about two weeks offline. "The investigation has been completed and the fix has been applied," the studio said. [2]
