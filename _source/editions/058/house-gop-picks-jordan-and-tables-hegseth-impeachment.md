House Republicans elected Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio as their leader for the next Congress on Tuesday, beating Majority Leader Steve Scalise 106 to 73 in a secret ballot, according to lawmakers in the room. Jordan, a founder of the Freedom Caucus and chair of the House Judiciary Committee, will lead the party in the minority after Democrats won control of both chambers in the midterms. [1]

Across the Capitol, Senate Republicans kept their whole leadership team, re-electing Sen. John Thune of South Dakota as leader by acclamation, with no dissent. [1][2]

The same day, the House voted 211 to 204 to refer a resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, which effectively shelves it. The resolution, led by outgoing Rep. Thomas Massie of Kentucky, targeted Hegseth's conduct in office and his handling of the war with Iran. Massie was the only Republican to vote against the referral. [2][3]

A Pentagon spokesman called the result "total victory" for Hegseth on social media. [3]
