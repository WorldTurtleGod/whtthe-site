James Gunn and Peter Safran are leaving their leadership jobs at DC Studios. Jeremy Latcham, whose credits include early Marvel films such as Iron Man, The Avengers, and Guardians of the Galaxy, becomes president and chief creative officer on December 1. He will report to the co-chairs of Skydance's film group. [1]

Skydance says Gunn and Safran will go back to creative work, including the Superman follow-up Man of Tomorrow, which is in postproduction. No project cancellations have been announced. [1]

Elsewhere on screens, Prime Video released the final trailer for Blade Runner 2099, a limited series starring Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer, set 50 years after Blade Runner 2049. All eight episodes arrive November 25, and Ridley Scott is an executive producer. [1]

Skydance also says a Paramount+ and HBO Max bundle is coming in early 2027. It has not given a price or exact date. [1]
