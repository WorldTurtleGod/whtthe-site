This is a sample article for the new What The? News site. It is not real news. The links below are examples of how sources appear, not citations of a real report.

A library that does not exist has opened a quiet wing for books that were never finished. The shelves, in this sample account, are full of first chapters, brave outlines, and one novel that stops, politely, in the middle of a sentence so the reader can catch a bus. Borrowers may take a manuscript home on the condition that they do not end it. [1]

A made-up librarian said the most popular item is a stack of blank pages labeled "Chapter Two, probably." The late-fee policy is simple: if you finish the book, you have to come back and talk about it. [2]

What The? News has not catalogued any unfinished novels, and no librarians were interviewed, because the wing is a device for this layout.
