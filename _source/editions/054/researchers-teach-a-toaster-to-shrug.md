A lab that does not exist says it has trained a toaster to answer difficult questions by lifting both slots a quarter inch and leaving them there. The gesture, researchers claim, is surprisingly versatile. Asked about the weather, the toaster shrugged. Asked about the future of work, the toaster shrugged. Asked whether the bread was ready, the toaster shrugged, and then, after a pause, shrugged again. [1]

The team published a sample paper with a blank results table and a single footnote: "Further work is needed, or possibly less." Funding, in this fictional account, arrived in the form of a very patient grant officer who also shrugged, which the lab took as peer review. [2]
