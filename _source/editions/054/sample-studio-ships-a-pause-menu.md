This is a sample article for the new What The? News site. It is not real news. The links below are examples of how sources appear, not citations of a real report.

In the made-up port town of Lower Save Point, a small studio has released a title with no levels, no battles, and no map. Players load in, press Start, and stay there. The pause menu, testers say, is unusually comfortable. The cursor sighs when it moves. The "Resume" button does not resume anything, because there is nothing to resume, and that appears to be the point. [1]

A made-up spokesperson for the studio, speaking from a folding chair, called the project "a place to sit down." Early players report losing an afternoon to the options screen, then quietly closing the lid and feeling fine about it. [2]

Critics in this sample universe are split. One camp wants a sequel with a second menu. The other camp wants the credits to roll now, while the feeling is still good. The studio has promised a patch that adds a "Quit" option, then delayed the patch, then added a note that quitting was always available, just one screen deeper than anyone looked.
