Most game trailers are built to show you the opening act. Playground Games just showed the middle of its story, and it seems pleased with itself for doing so.

On the Official Xbox Podcast, released November 13 as part of Xbox's 25th anniversary week, Playground's Ralph Fulton talked through a new three-minute Fable clip set in Deepwood, "the magical forest right in the middle of Albion." [1] Usually beautiful, he said, this stretch of it is "not so beautiful for reasons which are definitely relevant to the plot." [1]

Fulton placed the clip at the end of what the team calls Act 3, "probably getting on for halfway through the game." [1] It is also, he said, "literally the first time in the game that the name Isabel is mentioned at all." Isabel, played by Hayley Atwell, has starred in earlier trailers, but your Hero has never heard of her until this scene. From here, Fulton said, the hunt for her runs "right across Albion and ultimately right to the end of the game." [1][2]

The Hero's companion in the clip is Arden, played by Susan Wokoma, a self-described retired Hero who thinks "nobody should be a Hero" because the power is too much for one person. [1] The forest is full of cursed creatures glowing red. Fulton called them tougher, more durable versions of existing enemies that show up in parts of the world struck by the curse. [1][2]

He also explained why the story doesn't rush you. Your village and your grandmother are turned to stone early, but stone can be undone any time, so the game lets you wander, take a job as a blacksmith or go on a date in a pub without feeling foolish. The pressure only gets real around Acts 5 and 6, he said, "at which point you would be remiss if you were to be spending your time dating in a pub." [1]

Fulton said the team is "gliding into the final stages of our project here, fixing those final few pesky bugs." Host Joe Skrebels closed with the date: Fable is out February 23, 2027, and he teased more Fable news on Xbox Wire around the anniversary weekend. [1][2]

**Commentary.** It's a good sign that the new one trusts players to waste time. The best adventures have always had room for the side road.
