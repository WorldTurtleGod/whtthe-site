A team of botanists who appear only in this article have described a plant that looks ordinary until someone raises a camera, at which point it becomes, in their words, "a polite blur." Drawings work. Memory works. Photographs come out as a green smudge with the mood of a person who has just stepped behind a tree. [1]

The proposed name, offered here as decoration, is longer than the plant is tall. A field guide entry, also fictional, recommends sitting with the plant until you no longer want a picture. [2]
