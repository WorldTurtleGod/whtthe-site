HBO Max posted three character posters to Instagram at 6:00 a.m. PST on Monday, November 9, 2026, in a joint post with HBO, the official Harry Potter account and two other accounts. The new trio is Dominic McLaughlin as Harry Potter, Arabella Stanton as Hermione Granger, and Alastair Stout as Ron Weasley. [1][2]

The design is the argument. All three are tight close-ups in Hogwarts uniform against a warmly lit, book-lined library background, each holding a wand upright straight down the middle of the face. The tagline across the top is "The First Year Begins"; the bottom carries "HBO Original Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone," "New Series Dec 25" and the HBO Max logo. [2]

The series itself — Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone, an eight-episode first season — premieres Friday, December 25, 2026, on HBO and HBO Max. These posters follow the series' first poster, revealed October 29, 2026. The adult cast includes John Lithgow as Albus Dumbledore, Janet McTeer as Minerva McGonagall, Paapa Essiedu as Severus Snape, and Nick Frost as Rubeus Hagrid. Francesca Gardiner is showrunner; Mark Mylod directs multiple episodes. [1]

The reaction on X on November 9 was fast and mixed, and it is worth reporting as reaction, not consensus. Coverage in ComingSoon and That Park Place documents praise for the return to Hogwarts alongside criticism of the editing and unnatural appearance — including complaints that the two sides of each face look awkwardly combined, and renewed debate over Harry's blue (rather than book-green) eyes. One poster put it bluntly: "These are objectively awful lol." Another: "Everything I see from this show just looks like bad cosplay." [3][4]

This is a news story about a poster release, not a review: the series has not aired, so there is no verdict here on whether it works. What the posters do prove is simpler — a quarter-century after the first film, the argument over who Harry Potter is, and what he should look like, still starts the moment a new image drops.
