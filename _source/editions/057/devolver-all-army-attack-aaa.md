On Monday, November 9, Devolver Digital said on X that it was about to announce its first-ever "AAA" game. The next day it did — and the joke was the announcement. The game is All Army Attack, developed by BlackLight Games and published by Devolver, and "AAA" is the game's initials, not a big-budget label. Devolver remains an indie publisher; this is not a hundred-million-dollar blockbuster. [1][2]

What it actually is: a "chaotic, large-scale roguelike auto-battler" in which animals and plants unite against a formidable cosmic threat. The player is a Commander recruiting troops and artillery from a pool of cards. It comes to PC via Steam in 2027, with no exact launch date, price, or other platforms confirmed yet. [1]

The demo is the near-term news. A playable demo runs November 16–23, 2026, as part of Steam Auto-Battler Fest, covering the Prologue and the Arid Wastes zone across 6 escalating difficulty levels, with 3 playable characters, 60 unit types, 25 unit enhancements, 40 treasures, and more than 70 cards, plus a demo-exclusive Treasure Shop. [1][3]

One loose end, stated plainly: players can challenge friends online in the demo to compare armies, but the Steam store listing currently categorises the full game as single-player, so it is not yet clear whether that online feature carries into the final release. [4]

Devolver has built its name on exactly this kind of gag — a publisher that treats a reveal as a punchline and then ships the game underneath it. This time the punchline is the title, and the game underneath it is animals with artillery.
