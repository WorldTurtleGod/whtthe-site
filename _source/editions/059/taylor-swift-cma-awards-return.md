Taylor Swift is returning to the CMA Awards stage on Nov. 18 — her first CMA performance in 13 years. Good Morning America made the announcement on Friday, Nov. 13. Timing the news on Friday the 13th was fitting for a singer who considers 13 her lucky number. [1]

The appearance comes less than a month after Swift reflected on her Nashville beginnings during her Oct. 26 induction into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame, where she celebrated the 20th anniversary of her self-titled debut album. "You know, I've been very fortunate. I've gotten to genre hop and travel the world and write with so many different people and in so many different ways," she said in her acceptance speech. "And the only reason that I've been able to have that kind of dexterity in my creative process is because of the fundamentals and the foundation that I learned here in Nashville." [1]

She is not just performing. Swift earned a Single of the Year nomination for "I Knew It, I Knew You," her song from Disney and Pixar's Toy Story 5, written and produced with Jack Antonoff. The track reached No. 1 on country radio in September — nearly 12 years after she made her definitive move from country to pop with 1989. It is her first CMA nomination in four years. [1]

Swift has delivered eight musical performances across seven CMA Awards ceremonies, and she has earned 12 CMA honors, including the Pinnacle Award in 2013. Her last CMA performance was that same night, a stripped-down "Red" with Alison Krauss and Vince Gill. She has been back since — presenting entertainer of the year to Garth Brooks in 2016, winning song of the year as the writer of Little Big Town's "Better Man" in 2017 — but she has not sung on that stage in 13 years. [1]

Thirteen years is a long time to stay away from the room where you started. Nashville made her, she left it for the biggest pop career on the planet, and now she is coming back on the exact date her superstition would pick, with a country radio No. 1 in her pocket. The CMAs have not had a moment like this in a long time. On Wednesday, they will.
