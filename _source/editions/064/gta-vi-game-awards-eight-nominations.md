The Game Awards revealed its 2026 nominees on Tuesday, November 17, in a livestream hosted by Geoff Keighley, with the shortlists posted to X the same day [1][2]. There are six Game of the Year nominees, not five — a tie in the jury's unranked ballots for the last spot: 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Grand Theft Auto VI, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time [1].

Grand Theft Auto VI leads the whole field with eight nominations: Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, Best Action/Adventure, and Best Performance [1]. That count is a tally of the official category lists — HUDWire's count, which Variety's matches — not a number the Awards itself publishes [1]. Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem follow with six each, and Phantom Blade Zero has five [1].

The timing is the story. GTA VI launches Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — tomorrow [1]. It is eligible because the Awards' rules require games to be "commercially available to customers on or before Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 p.m. PT," and the rules let publishers give jury outlets advance, pre-release access — so the jurors who nominated it judged review code, not a game the public has played [1]. Keighley told Variety the cutoff was not moved for GTA: "our dates have been very set every year" [1].

The Best Performance nomination carries its own news. It went to Manni L. Perez for GTA VI; the Awards does not name her role. VGC reports the nomination confirms she plays Lucia; Polygon calls it all but confirmed, and Rockstar has not officially named any of the cast [1]. The other Performance nominees are Angela Sant'Albano (Resident Evil Requiem), Patrick Gibson (007 First Light), Liam McIntyre (Marvel's Wolverine) and Rahul Kohli (Saros) [1]. The official @thegameawards account announced the top category on X on November 17: "Here are your six nominees for Game of the Year. #TheGameAwards streaming live on Thursday, December 10." [1]

Winners are announced Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. ET from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles [1]. Fan voting is open now and closes December 9 at 6 p.m. PT — and fans should know their weight going in: the result is 90 percent jury, 10 percent fan vote [1]. For the record, the last Rockstar game at this show, Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018, won Narrative, Score and Performance and lost Game of the Year to God of War [1].

Commentary: Judge the eligibility rule fairly — it is old, it is written down, and it was not bent for Rockstar. But sit with what it produces: the biggest game of the decade arrives pre-crowned as the year's most honored title, judged by professionals on code the audience hasn't touched, a day before the audience gets its say at the register. This paper's standing rule applies: when the sales number is good, they lead with sales. Tomorrow, watch what Rockstar leads with.
