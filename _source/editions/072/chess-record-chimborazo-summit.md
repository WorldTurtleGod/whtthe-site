Guinness World Records published the record on November 24: best friends Adam Wininger, 32, and Joshua Host, 44, both of the USA, hold the title for highest altitude playing a board/party game [1]. They earned it on Chimborazo in Bolivar, Ecuador, on March 31, playing chess at 6,277 meters — 20,593 feet [1]. Print the dates straight: the climb was in March; the record's publication this week is the news.

The mountain is a record-holder itself — Chimborazo holds the record for farthest mountain peak from Earth's centre [1]. The pair had attempted it a few months earlier and could not summit because of weather [1]. This time they made it, in cold severe enough that both men wore gloves for the match [1]. Adam won the game; Guinness notes he is a trained ultramarathon athlete, which gave him a slight advantage at altitude [1].

The margin was 200 feet. The previous record, 6,216 meters (20,393 feet), was set by Tim Maw and Neil Laughton of the UK on Putha Hiunchuli in Nepal on September 17, 2024 [1].

Two friends dreamed of breaking a world record together, picked the game they loved and the mountain that had already turned them back once, and carried a chess board to the top of it. No sponsor stunt, no audience — just thin air, gloves, and a game that got harder to think through with every move. Some records exist because somebody needed the number. This one exists because two friends wanted to see if they could. The answer is in the book.
