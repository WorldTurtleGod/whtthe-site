Horstman's Cleaners and Furriers reopened in Carbondale on Tuesday, October 27, after more than seven months closed [1]. The shop at 303 S. University Ave. closed in March after owner Jon Kirk slipped on ice outside the business in February, suffered multiple ankle fractures, and went through surgery and rehabilitation that left him wheelchair dependent for a time [1].

A city grant put in the new boiler the shop needed. "I just now got back up, and was able to get a grant from the City of Carbondale … put in my new machinery that I needed, and so we're back open now," Kirk said [1]. On Thursday, November 19, the Carbondale Chamber of Commerce hosted a grand reopening ribbon-cutting, and Kirk has brought back his employees while reducing his own hours after the injury [2].

The shop's roots are the kind no chain can fake. Horstman's opened in Du Quoin, moved to Carbondale in December 1925, and has sat at its current location since 1964 [1]. It has been in Kirk's family since 1965 [1]. Kirk began working there in 1977 while he was in junior high school and took over operations in 1995 [1].

What he missed was not the machinery. "Waiting on people, talking to people, building a relationship, meeting people, and then knowing them, so, you get to know their families," he said [1]. City Manager Stan Reno, a longtime patron, said he was "just very excited to have a long-standing family business back open, providing some essential services to the community" [1].

At the ribbon-cutting, Kirk kept it simple: "I'm just overwhelmed. All the support and it's been in my family, 60 years, so to come back it's just great, absolutely great" [2]. A man, a patch of ice, seven lost months, and a town that showed up when the sign flipped back to open. That is the whole story, and it is enough.
