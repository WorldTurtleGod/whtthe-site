Capcom has set the date. Service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of 8:00 PM (PDT) on Wednesday, September 29, 2027 [1]. After that, in Capcom's own words, "Exoprimal will no longer be playable after the end of service" [1].

The wind-down is already moving. The game's four Survival Pass Premium Tiers stopped being sold on November 24, 2026, and the game, its DLC, and all related products leave stores on March 30, 2027 [1]. Owners can re-download the game before service ends [1]. That is a stay of execution, not a pardon.

The disc does not save you either. Exoprimal got a physical release, but it requires an internet connection to servers in order to play, so it will stop functioning whether you own it physically or digitally once those servers close [3]. You bought a box. The box phones home. When home stops answering, the box is a coaster.

Players are reading the notice the way players do. Exoprimal holds a "mostly positive" user review rating on Steam based on more than 1,700 English-language reviews, but recent reviews submitted after the shutdown notice have been much more negative [2]. Push Square noted on November 25 that the game was still full price on the PS Store [3]. A death notice and a full-price tag on the same shelf tells you how the storefront sees the remaining fans: as a last harvest.

Capcom's announcement is polite. It thanks players and apologizes "for the gravity of this announcement" [1]. Gravity is the right word. A game released in July 2023 [2] gets four years of life, then deletion — not an offline mode, not a patch to set it free, just a date. The dinosaurs were never the extinct ones. The purchase was.
