The setup fits in one line from the trailer: "I lied to my dad that I was doing a hospital internship and he has no idea I'm here." [3]

Paramount Pictures released the official trailer for K-Pop: The Debut on Monday, Nov. 23. [2][4] The film follows Lizzy Lee, a Korean-American woman played by Ji-young Yoo, who has spent her life on the path her family picked for her. When she wins a spot in a globally televised competition to form the next K-pop girl group, she flies to Seoul and keeps the whole thing secret. Performers from around the world are competing for "just four coveted spots," according to Paramount's synopsis. [1]

In the trailer, Lizzy is about to start medical school. "Are you ready to start med school?" someone asks her, and she laughs. Friends push her to tape an audition on the spot. "So I turned down med school just to get humiliated on a global scale?" she says later. "I don't have a plan B." [3] ComingSoon reported that her audition tape features BTS member Jin's song "Epiphany." [2]

Singer Eric Nam plays Kevin, the "unexpected ally" in Paramount's synopsis, who helps her find her confidence. [1][2] The cast also includes Tony Revolori, Gia Kim, Yim Si-wan and Yoo Ji-tae, who appeared in Oldboy, according to Teen Vogue. [4]

The cameos are a K-pop fan's checklist. Teen Vogue reported that the trailer opens with members of KATSEYE announcing the auditions for the show-within-the-film, "K-pop Superstar." SEVENTEEN's Joshua Hong plays the driver who picks Lizzy up in Seoul. ENHYPEN's Jay and Jake, 2NE1's Dara, Mamamoo's Hwasa and Tablo of Epik High appear as judges. [4] ComingSoon also lists ILLIT. [2] The soundtrack includes BTS, TWICE, aespa, IVE, LE SSERAFIM and BLACKPINK's Jisoo, according to ComingSoon. FirstShowing adds CORTIS. [2][5]

The sources differ on the trailer's music. Teen Vogue says it is set to "Gnarly" before Jin's "Epiphany" takes over. ComingSoon describes "Epiphany" as the song on Lizzy's audition tape. [2][4]

Behind the camera, Korean-American filmmaker Benson Lee directs, from a screenplay by Elyse Hollander and Eileen Shim. Lee also made Planet B-Boy and Seoul Searching. [2][5] FirstShowing reported that the story is based on Alina Simone's article "So You Want to Be a K-Pop Star." [5] HYBE America, the U.S. arm of BTS's label, and Epic Magazine produced the film with Paramount and Domain Entertainment. [2][4] The Korean title is 케이팝 슈퍼스타, "K-Pop Superstar." [5]

K-Pop: The Debut opens in U.S. theaters Feb. 26, 2027. [1][4]

**Commentary.** This is what real cultural exchange looks like. No State Department program, no grant and no summit communique. A Korean label and an American studio made a movie about a Korean-American kid chasing a dream in Seoul because millions of Americans want to watch it. South Korea is one of America's few real allies in Asia, and the most lasting part of that alliance may be stories like this one that families on both sides share. The film's only open question is the one fans keep asking: whether a glossy studio picture will show the grind of the trainee system or just the stage lights.
