1. baaja-cloture-65-31-final-gate (LEAD, Power, Juno)
Scene: the same stone stairway from the last two days' art, now seen from just past the third and final tall gate, which stands open behind the bound bill on its hand truck. Ahead, the path continues to a plain closed door at the top with no clock on it. To one side, the small stone circle and earthen mound from yesterday sit undisturbed, with a folded, unopened envelope lying on the ground next to them, not picked up.
Key detail: all the gates are passed, but the final door is still shut and has no time on it (final passage not scheduled), and the left-behind envelope (the tribal fix that never got a vote).
Mood: momentum, but unfinished; flat midday light.
Leave out: real senators or faces, Native caricature or headdresses, party symbols, flags, the Capitol dome, numbers, text.

2. gta-vi-launch-99-estimate-not-sales (Games, Juno)
Scene: a midnight launch. A game box glows on a pedestal with a big, solid, heavy trophy-like seal beside it (the score). Next to it, a cash register's paper tape reads blank, and a translucent ghost-outline stack of coins hovers above the counter, dotted-line and see-through, not solid.
Key detail: the solid, real review score next to a see-through, estimated pile of money (sales are only a third-party estimate).
Mood: neon night, celebratory with a skeptical wink.
Leave out: GTA/Rockstar logos or characters, readable numbers or text, real people's faces.

3. outage-thread-upgrade-not-attacker (Tech & AI, Juno)
Scene: a long row of old, heavy machines (a server rack, an airport control-tower console, a bank vault terminal) linked by one tangled cable. A worker's gloved hand is in the middle of swapping one old router for a shiny new one, and sparks and dark screens spread down the line from that spot. No hooded hacker anywhere.
Key detail: the failure starts at the upgrade, at the hand doing the swap, not with an attacker.
Mood: tense, dim control-room light, clumsy rather than sinister.
Leave out: hackers, skulls, hooded figures, agency or company logos, text.

4. simple-pleasures-cafe-wheatville-church (Culture, Juno)
Scene: inside a small old country church turned café at early morning. An ornate patterned tin ceiling overhead, a short counter with a griddle, a couple of stuffed easy chairs in a corner, and a new wooden ramp and railing at the front door. Two figures seen from behind (a man at the griddle, a woman setting out sandwiches).
Key detail: the original pressed-tin ceiling and the church shape of the building, now a working café.
Mood: warm, homemade, hopeful, early-light glow through tall arched windows.
Leave out: crosses or religious icons as the focus, signs or text, recognizable faces.

6. bogong-moth-magnetic-compass-nature (Science, Juno)

Key detail: the field lines are tilted (the moth reads the tilt of the field), and the moth heads toward a far-off cave.
Mood: quiet wonder, deep night, high contrast.
Leave out: compasses with letters, text, cartoon faces on the moth.
