1. baaja-passes-senate-62-33 (LEAD, Power, Juno)
Scene: the same stone stairway and gates from the last three days' art. The bound bill on its hand truck has gone through the plain door at the top, which now stands open behind it. The bill sits on a narrow bridge leading across to a second, separate building in mist: a new, steeper stairway whose top can't be seen. The crowd of figures that pushed it up the stairs is visibly thinner than before, a few shapes walking away down the steps. A small hourglass sits on the bridge railing, sand running low.
Key detail: it got through, but with fewer pushers at every step, and now it faces a second, foggier climb (the House) with time running out (funding ends Dec. 11).
Mood: earned win, but uncertain; late-afternoon light.
Leave out: real senators or faces, party symbols, flags, the Capitol dome, readable numbers, text.

2. isaac-repentance-plus-console-launch (Games, Juno)
Scene: a cozy couch at night with four game controllers held by four unseen players (hands only), their cords and wireless glow all leading to one shared TV showing an abstract dark basement dungeon of round rooms. On the coffee table, an open game case with a real, chunky physical cartridge sitting in it, plus a small instruction booklet and a little keychain-strap charm.
Key detail: a real cartridge in the box (the whole game, not a download key) and four people playing together online.
Mood: warm, gleeful, a little spooky-cute.
Leave out: Isaac/Nintendo/PlayStation/Xbox logos or the actual Isaac character art, text.

3. anthropic-record-ipo-existential-warning (Tech & AI, Juno)
Scene: a grand stock-exchange stage with a huge celebratory confetti burst and a bell, a towering stack of gold coins rising behind the podium. In the foreground on the podium lies an open, thick legal prospectus; one page in it shows a small, plain drawing of a cracked globe, the only dark, quiet thing in the bright scene.
Key detail: the record-money celebration and, in the company's own filing, a warning about catastrophic risk to humanity.
Mood: dazzling, with an unsettling undertone.
Leave out: real people's faces (no Dario Amodei likeness), Anthropic/Claude/Nasdaq logos, ticker symbols, numbers, text.

4. brass-rail-port-huron-reopens (Culture, Juno)
Scene: an old 1930s-style neighborhood bar on a downtown street just after opening, warm light pouring out, booths and a long polished bar filled with people of all ages greeting each other with hugs and handshakes. Behind the bar, a vintage framed photo of a woman behind this same bar decades ago (generic, not a real likeness), and a brass foot rail running the length of the bar.
Key detail: a family landmark reopened and instantly packed, with the founder's era remembered on the wall.
Mood: homecoming, warm and lively.
Leave out: brand logos, beer labels, readable signs, text.

5. garibaldi-liberty-43-seconds (Strange, Juno)
Scene: a performance painter, seen from behind and mid-motion, flipping a big canvas upside-down on an outdoor waterfront stage with a suspension bridge in the background and a crowd cheering. On the canvas, as it turns, a bold, loose-brushstroke face of the Statue of Liberty with its crown spikes emerges right-side up. Paint pots and a stopwatch on a stool nearby.
Key detail: the flip of the canvas revealing Lady Liberty, and the stopwatch (it took under a minute).
Mood: showy, joyful, kinetic.
Leave out: a recognizable face for the painter, Guinness logos, readable time digits, text.

6. kuiper-belt-heavy-water-dust (Science, Juno)
Scene: split composition. Left: a high-altitude research jet with very long straight wings flying at the edge of space, with a few tiny glinting dust grains streaming toward a collector under its wing. Right: one of those grains magnified huge, a craggy, layered particle with clay-like sheets and tiny frozen droplets locked inside, and behind it, far off, a dim ring of icy bodies beyond a small distant planet.
Key detail: a speck of dust caught in Earth's sky that carries water from beyond Neptune.
Mood: wonder, quiet and cold.
Leave out: NASA logos, labels, text.

8. rollins-religious-emails-buck-stops (Power, Publius)
Scene: a big open-plan government office where every worker's computer screen glows with the same message: a simple envelope icon opening to show a small church window shape. At the front, a judge's gavel has come down on a large mail-sorting chute, stopping the flow of envelopes. A simple sign-like shape (no text) of a rulebook sits open next to the gavel.
Key detail: one boss's religious message pushed to every screen in the department, and a court halting it under her own rules.
Mood: firm, orderly, matter-of-fact (not mocking faith).
Leave out: any likeness of Brooke Rollins, USDA logos, crosses as mockery, real faces, text.

10. later-generations-keep-memory-longer (Science, Publius)
Scene: three generations side by side on a park bench (grandparent, parent, younger adult), each with a gentle glowing thread of floating small memory objects (photos, keys, a bicycle) above their head. The oldest's thread fades and drops away sooner; the younger ones' threads stay longer and brighter, shown by length and brightness, not hue.
Key detail: later-born people keeping their memories longer.
Mood: hopeful, soft afternoon light.
Leave out: brain-scan gore, medical logos, text, numbers.

11. ogunquit-tin-roosters-not-ads (Strange, Publius)
Scene: a seaside New England house with a cozy breakfast café attached. On the lawn, two six-foot tin roosters are chained to the front porch pillars, one draped in a blanket. A small girl peeks out from behind a rooster's big tin tail. At the curb, a town official's clipboard and a stack of fine envelopes; next door, an identical tin rooster stands on a neighbor's lawn, untouched.
Key detail: the same rooster that is "art" next door is cited as an "ad" here.
Mood: absurd, small-town, sympathetic to the family.
Leave out: the girl's face in detail (keep her small and from behind or partly hidden), restaurant name or logos, readable signs, text.
