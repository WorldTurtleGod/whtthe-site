Cal State Fullerton still has not posted its own 2027 softball schedule. So the slate is arriving the way scouting usually does — one opponent's announcement at a time. Since this section's Oklahoma advance look ran yesterday, here is the confirmed shape of February, with each fixture graded for how solid it is.

**Documented: Tarleton State vs. Fullerton, Thursday, February 19, 2027, 7 p.m., Puerto Vallarta.** Tarleton State's official schedule announcement of November 13 closes its Puerto Vallarta College Challenge run "against Cal State Fullerton on Feb. 19 at 7 p.m." at Nancy Almaraz Stadium [1]. The Texans are part of the event's second session, February 16–20, alongside Duke, Connecticut, Miami (Ohio) and Fullerton [1]. That is an opponent's official release with a date and a time — the strongest grade a fixture gets before Fullerton's own page moves.

**Opponent graphic: Texas A&M at Fullerton, Thursday, February 11, 2027, 7 p.m., Easton Classic.** Texas A&M Softball's official Easton Classic graphic, posted November 8 on its verified Instagram and Facebook, lists "Feb. 11, 7 p.m. vs. Fullerton" in a six-game Easton slate at Anderson Family Field [2][3]. If it holds, that is Fullerton's season opener, a day before Oklahoma. The caveat stays on it in print: no Texas A&M schedule page confirming the game has been found yet, so this one is an official graphic, not a published schedule line.

**One line of recap, not a re-run:** Oklahoma at Fullerton, Thursday, February 12, 2027, 7 p.m., Easton Classic — the fixture yesterday's advance look scouted from Oklahoma's official schedule release [4]. It is not repeated here; the scouting picture stands as printed.

**Confirmed opponent, date to be announced: Indiana State.** Indiana State's official November 12 schedule announcement names Fullerton among its Easton Classic contests but gives no date or time for that game [5]. It is printed here as confirmed-opponent/TBA. This paper does not guess dates.

**Field only:** the Judi Garman Classic, March 4–7, 2027, carries a 12-team field announced by Titans Softball on November 11 — UCLA, Florida, UCF, Arizona, Duke, San Jose State, Cal State Bakersfield, Utah State, Seattle U, California Baptist, Weber State and Fullerton. No Fullerton matchup or time has been announced, so none is printed.

No forecast accompanies any of this — there are no 2027 rosters or stats to forecast from, and this section does not invent fixtures, results or numbers. Fullerton is the team this section follows; Texas A&M, Tarleton State and Indiana State appear here only as Fullerton opponents.
