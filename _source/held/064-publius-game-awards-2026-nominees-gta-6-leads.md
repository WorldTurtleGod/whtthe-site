The Game Awards announced its 2026 nominees on Tuesday, Nov. 17, in a livestream hosted by creator Geoff Keighley on YouTube and Twitch. The game with the most nominations is one nobody outside the press has played. [1][2]

Grand Theft Auto VI comes out Thursday, Nov. 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It picked up eight nominations: Game of the Year, Best Action/Adventure, Best Art Direction, Best Audio Design, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Score & Music, and Best Performance for Manni L. Perez. [3][4]

The six Game of the Year nominees are 007 First Light (IO Interactive), Control Resonant (Remedy/Annapurna), Grand Theft Auto VI (Rockstar Games), Phantom Blade Zero (S-GAME), Resident Evil Requiem (Capcom) and Nintendo's remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. [1][2] Resident Evil Requiem and Control Resonant each landed six nominations, according to Crunchyroll's count and Xbox's own nominee list. [3][4]

**How a game that isn't out qualifies.** Variety reported that GTA 6 slips in just under the eligibility window, which closes Friday, Nov. 20. Some fans claimed the show moved its dates to fit Rockstar. Keighley told Variety that isn't true: "our dates have been very set every year. It's the same calendar that we have." He added that he is "sure some of these companies" plan their release dates around the show. [1]

Nominees are picked by a jury of more than 100 media outlets and creators, and winners are decided by a mix of jury and fan votes. Fan voting is open now at TheGameAwards.com. The show is Thursday, Dec. 10, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, at 4:30 p.m. PT. [1][2]

**The AI question.** The other name to watch is Big Walk, from House House and publisher Panic. It is up for Best Independent Game, Best Family Game, Best Multiplayer and Games for Impact. [1] Earlier this month, House House said in an updated FAQ that it used AI chatbots "sparingly" to work through bugs late in development, and estimated that "well under 0.1%" of the game's code had anything to do with it. Its main example was a Unity shadow fix of "about 10 lines of code." The studio called its lack of an internal AI policy "a failure" and apologized. Game Informer reported the disclosure came after a group of Independent Games Festival jurors said they would not vote for any game that used generative AI "in any capacity." [5][6] Polygon wrote that the flap "could spell trouble" for Big Walk if Game Awards voters object to the technology. [2]

**Other categories.** Best RPG nominees are Final Fantasy Resonance, Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave, Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection, The Blood of Dawnwalker, and Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter. Best Adaptation nominees are Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2, Fallout Season 2, Mortal Kombat II, the live-action Resident Evil, and The Super Mario Galaxy Movie. Most Anticipated includes Fable, Final Fantasy VII Revelation, God of War Laufey, Resident Evil Veronica and The Witcher 4. [1]

**Commentary.** A Game of the Year nomination for a game no voter has finished is a bet on a brand, not a review of a game. GTA 6 may well deserve it. It may also turn out to be the best game of the decade. But the jury nominated it on trailers, previews and Rockstar's track record, and fans are right to say so. Meanwhile, a small studio is taking a beating over a sliver of chatbot-assisted bug fixing that it put in writing. If the industry wants honest awards, it should judge the games that are actually on shelves, and it should not punish the studios that tell the truth harder than the ones that stay quiet.
