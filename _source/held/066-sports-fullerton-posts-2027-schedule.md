The wait this section has printed three editions running is over. Cal State Fullerton announced its 2027 softball schedule on Thursday morning, November 19 — a 54-game slate with 28 games at Anderson Family Field, against a field that includes 12 teams that played in the 2026 NCAA Tournament [1].

Head coach Gina Oaks Garcia, a two-time Big West Coach of the Year entering her third season, did not schedule gently. "We intentionally challenged ourselves with some of the top programs in the country because we believe that competition will prepare us to make a deeper postseason run," she said [1].

The opening weekend is the Easton Classic, February 11–13, and Fullerton's own posting now confirms every fixture this section assembled from the other dugouts — and settles the one that was left open. Thursday, February 11 brings two games: Texas A&M, confirmed Wednesday on the Aggies' own schedule page, and Indiana State — which Edition 65 printed as confirmed-opponent with no date, and which Fullerton has now dated itself [1][2]. Friday, February 12 is Oklahoma, the fixture the Edition 63 advance look scouted [1]. Saturday, February 13 closes the weekend against Michigan State and Kentucky [1].

Then the slate gets rude, fast. Fullerton's first midweek game, on February 16, is against Texas — the 2026 Women's College World Series champions [1]. The Titans then travel to Mexico for the Puerto Vallarta College Challenge, February 18–21, facing Miami (Ohio), Duke, Tarleton State and UConn, and continue to the Mary Nutter Collegiate Classic, February 25–28 in Cathedral City [1]. They host the Judi Garman Classic March 4–7, with matchups against UCF, Florida, Seattle U, Arizona and Weber State [1]. UCF's own announcement the same day confirms the Knights return to the Judi Garman and face Fullerton on March 4 [3].

The rest of the shape: further home midweeks against Hawai'i (Feb. 23), Penn State (Mar. 10), Eastern Kentucky (Mar. 17), Loyola Marymount (Mar. 30) and San Diego (Apr. 27), plus one midweek trip to UCLA on April 20 [1]. Big West play opens at UC Riverside, March 19–21; the home conference opener is UC Santa Barbara, March 26–27, with further home series against CSUN, Cal Baptist and Cal State Bakersfield, and the regular season closes at Cal Poly, May 7–8 [1]. The Big West Championship runs May 12–15 at Long Beach State, with the top six teams earning berths [1]. Season and single-game tickets go on sale in December, and the program's Leadoff Social is set for January 22 [1].

One small signing note from the same week: outfielder Baylie Ciremele of Escondido, from Classical Academy, was announced as signed by the program's social accounts on November 18 — a social-grade announcement, printed here as exactly that [4].

No forecast accompanies any of this — there are still no 2027 rosters or stats to forecast from, and this section does not invent them. What changed is simpler: the slate is no longer assembled from opponents' graphics and releases. It is Fullerton's own, in Fullerton's own voice, and February now has a shape — two games on opening day, Oklahoma on Friday, and the defending champions four days later.
