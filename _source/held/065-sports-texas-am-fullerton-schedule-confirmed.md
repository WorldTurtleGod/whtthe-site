One upgrade, reported for exactly what it is. On Wednesday this section printed Texas A&M at Cal State Fullerton — Thursday, February 11, 2027, 7 p.m., Easton Classic at Anderson Family Field — sourced to Texas A&M Softball's official Easton Classic graphic, with the caveat in print that no schedule page confirmed it [1].

That caveat can now come off. Texas A&M's schedule page on 12thman.com lists the game directly: "Thu Feb 11 7:00 PM — Texas A&M at Cal State Fullerton — Anderson Family Field, Fullerton, Calif." [1] The page carries its own standing note that dates are subject to change, and no publication date is shown on it, so this paper reports the confirmation, not a publication day [1].

Nothing else moved. Indiana State's game against Fullerton remains confirmed-opponent with no date or time — Indiana State's November 12 announcement still gives none, and this paper does not guess one [2]. Fullerton's own 2027 schedule remains unposted, and no new Fullerton fixture, result or schedule announcement dated November 18–19 was found [3]. No forecast accompanies any of this — there are no 2027 rosters or stats to forecast from.
