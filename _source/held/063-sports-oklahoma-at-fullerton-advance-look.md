**ADVANCE LOOK — 2026 NUMBERS.** Everything below is Oklahoma's final 2026 season. Rosters turn over, and no 2027 stats exist for anyone yet, so there is no forecast in this piece — just the scouting picture, labeled for what it is.

Fullerton's own 2027 schedule still has not been posted. The first confirmed date on the Titans' slate arrived from the other dugout: Oklahoma's official 2027 schedule release of November 12 has the Sooners at Cal State Fullerton on Thursday, February 12, 2027, at 7 p.m. at Anderson Family Field, in the Easton Classic [1][2]. The schedule wrinkle matters: OU plays Utah at 5 p.m. the same day, so Fullerton gets the Sooners in the second game of a doubleheader [1].

What is coming to Fullerton, by the 2026 numbers: a 52–10 team that went 20–4 in the SEC and won the SEC regular-season championship, scored 615 runs, hit 187 home runs — the second-most in NCAA history — batted .384 as a team and fielded .981 [3][4]. Its season ended at home, in its own Super Regional, against unseeded Mississippi State — Oklahoma's first miss of the Women's College World Series since 2015 [3].

The top five all return in 2027. Catcher Kendall Wells hit .358 with 39 home runs — the NCAA single-season freshman record — and 88 RBI [4]. Center fielder Kai Minor hit a team-high .444 [3][4]. Right fielder Ella Parker hit .405 with 22 home runs [4]. Shortstop Gabbie Garcia hit .395 with 22 home runs [4]. And left-hander Audrey Lowry returns in the circle with a 2.70 ERA and 82 strikeouts [3][4]. (Two official OU sources conflict on Lowry's win-loss and innings lines, so this paper is not printing either — the ERA and strikeouts agree in both.)

The losses are real but bounded. Oklahoma has lost 59 of its 187 home runs: Isabela Emerling (.404, 20 HR) is out of eligibility, and Kasidi Pickering (.370, 20 HR) transferred to Texas Tech [3]. The Sooners still return 128 home runs and all four of their 2026 All-Americans [3].

And Fullerton has already stood in against this team. On February 19, 2026, Oklahoma beat the Titans 6–5 in 8 innings — after Fullerton had OU down to its final out [3]. That is the documented template: stay close, make the depth arms and the defense work, and be there in the eighth.

One honest note on the fall: Fullerton played fall exhibitions in October and November 2026 but published no scores or stats from them, so there are no fall numbers to scout — anyone quoting you some is making them up.
